Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Summary

PureUnlocker runs entirely on your own computer. It does not collect, transmit or store any personal data, and it contains no analytics, advertising or telemetry code.

1. Information we do not collect

The Software does not collect your name, email address, IP address, file contents, file names, browsing activity, or any usage statistics. It does not require an account and does not communicate with any server as part of its normal operation.

2. Information collected at purchase

When you buy the Software, our payment provider — which acts as the merchant of record — collects the information needed to process your payment and to meet tax and accounting obligations. This may include your name, email address, billing address and payment details. That information is handled under the payment provider’s own privacy policy, not this one.

3. Licence activation

The Software may verify your licence key. Any such check is limited to confirming that the key is valid and does not transmit information about your files or system.

4. Data storage

The Software stores its settings and licence information locally on your device. No data is uploaded.

5. Third-party services

The only third party involved in your purchase is our payment provider, used solely to process the transaction.

6. Children

The Software is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and revise the date above.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to support@aibaocai.com.
